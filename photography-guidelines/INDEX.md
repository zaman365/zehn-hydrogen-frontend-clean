## 🎯 Quick Specs Summary

### Product Images (Critical)
- **Dimensions:** 1200×1200px
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (Square)
- **Background:** Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- **File Size:** Max 200KB

### Hero Banners
- **Desktop:** 2400×1600px (3:2)
- **Mobile:** 1200×1600px (3:4)
- **File Size:** Max 500KB

### Category Tiles
- **Dimensions:** 1600×1600px (1:1)
- **File Size:** Max 300KB

### Bento Grid
- **Tall:** 1200×1800px (2:3)
- **Wide:** 1600×900px (16:9)
- **Square:** 1200×1200px (1:1)
